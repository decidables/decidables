## Dabble with Explorable Explanations of Decision Making

Welcome to :term[<span class="dec-name">dab’le</span>], where you can use interactive tasks,
manipulable diagrams, dynamic tables, and live equations to explore theories of decision making.
What you won't find here are detailed explanations of the tasks, measures, variables, and concepts
that underlie these theories. For that, please head to the full explorables available on the main
[<span class="decidables">decidables</span>](../) site.
